04 — BETTER STRUCTURE

Give your content
a clear structure.

Semantic HTML gives content meaning and helps browsers and assistive technologies understand the purpose of each section.

index.html 
<header>
  <nav aria-label="Main navigation">
    <a href="#home">Home</a>
  </nav>
</header>

<main id="main-content">
  <section>
    <h1>Welcome to my website</h1>
    <p>Useful and meaningful content.</p>
  </section>
</main>

<footer>
  <p>Thanks for visiting!</p>
</footer>
